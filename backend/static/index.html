Research Framework
How the website implements the two-phase thesis methodology.

  

    
    
    

      
Phase 1
Computational Analysis & Behavioural Modelling

        
Research data → preprocessing → EDA → behavioural risk indicators → tripartite EGT replicator dynamics.

      
Phase 2
Experimental Identification & Intelligent Recognition

        
Image/video → YOLO detection + ByteTrack → rule-based unsafe-behaviour identification → temporal events →
           HIRARC risk classification → validation against ground truth.

      
Integration
One monitoring platform

        
Phase-2 outputs (Unsafe Behaviour Rate, detector recall) calibrate Phase-1 EGT parameters; Phase-1 indicators
           justify the severity weights used in Phase-2 risk classification.

    

    
Preliminary research prototype — results are for academic demonstration and are not a certified safety system.
